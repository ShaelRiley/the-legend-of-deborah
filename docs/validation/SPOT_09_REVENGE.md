## Implemented contract

`damsel_revenge` is a three-unit Throwable utility: hold and use either mouse
button, no projectile, Magic, ammunition, wallet debit or class roll. Exactly one
unit is staged through Equipment.Consume and committed synchronously with the
existing 0.6-second cooldown after repeated exact held item/weapon/Hero/run/clock
admission. Missing generation, failed debit, stale callbacks and invalid uses
spend nothing. One accepted activation is shared by the entire current encounter.
It survives the activating Hero's death/disconnection, not reset or campaign failure;
it does not allow another use, gun reroll, replacement or refund.

A named encounter stream uniformly selects Pistol, SMG or Pulse Rifle and freezes
one ordinary procedural current-dungeon item through Equipment.Generate. Shotgun,
Revolver, Wand, Crowbar, alternate fire and grenades are excluded. The rare utility
uses a separate 1/16 conversion of only equipment-eligible Healing Potion results
remaining after Card, Feather, Hourglass, bomb and Chest Key conversion. Every
preceding outcome and fixed supply remains unchanged. No new equipment affix law.

The existing protected, stationary `lod_deborah` remains the source, with no
combatant HP, class, feat, navigation, summon slot or enemy-target membership.
Support uses the selected canonical firearm's base dice through CombatRolls,
shared physical defenses and ordinary native damage/death settlement. No source
Hero stats, feats, active equipment, ammunition or kill-credit inheritance. The
frozen item's properties are inactive until a Hero normally collects/equips it.
No bonus XP, reward pool, projectile body or hostile actor is added.

Acquire only the exact living visible real Gordon of the committed court, through
FactionManager and exact generated-cell membership. At least one living deployed
Hero must remain in the court. No fakes, turrets, Hector, Heroes or other hostile
damage. First-body traces block without friendly damage. Cloak, cover, no target
and freeze prevent firing without catch-up, healing or reroll. The normal entry
alcove and arrival sanctuary remain bilateral. Activating from the alcove fails.

The exact closed jail slab alone exposes a centered visible firing slit, width
min(160, GateWidth - 8), height 64, center 52 units over its floor. Both ray/slab-face
intersections must fit with four-unit edge clearance. Other walls, bodies, floors,
headers, side exits and doors remain blockers. Server native door collision,
progression lock and navigation are unchanged: Heroes cannot walk or fire through
this port. Client rendering draws its four surrounding panels from matching
shared dimensions and a short reciprocal ownership lease.

Existing Warden service schedules this single encounter effect at no faster than
0.2 seconds. Arming delay is 0.6 seconds; minimum Pistol/SMG/Pulse Rifle shot gaps
are 0.6/0.3/0.4 seconds, rounded upward by service cadence, never caught up. Range
is four Maze.CellSize units and muzzle height is 52; tolerated source/packet drift
is four units. One client-only stock gun and four owned arm poses use a 0.6-second
lease, exact actor/door ownership, bounded recoil and source-safe pose restoration.
The provisional consumable visual is a red-magenta stock clipboard with a distinct
code-native icon. Native pose, sightline, material, sound and readability are not
certified by the renderer doubles.

Exact RunManager state, campaign epoch/seed/run ID, dungeon/seed, graph/progression/
arena, Warden/native boss progression/status life, Damsel target/entity/source life,
stationary origin and jail identity bind work. The single-use native damage receipt

one frozen gun to drop at her feet. Existing LootDirector creates and owns the
individualized pickup outside Touch/Use. It belongs to the successful activating

Damsel Touch/Use forwards to that same deferred pickup claim when her own solid
