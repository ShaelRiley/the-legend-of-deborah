# B20 exposure evidence and retained failures

B19's exact 512-plan dataset and weighting history remain in
BESTIARY_B19_EXPOSURE.md. B20 replaces duplicate-ticket weighting with dungeon
motifs and committed campaign memory. The old independent-floor sample still
runs all geometric and composition invariants and prints comparable counts;
its per-identity frequency assertions move to the mandatory32x20 sequential
campaign gate, retaining25 planned/20 legal/5 early and adding36/54 coverage in
**every** campaign. This is an explicit population change, not evidence that
B19's old numeric counts remain unchanged. No seeds or exposure floors were
changed to rescue an individual identity.

The initial campaign harness inherited omission of four legacy template modules
(Deadcrab, Bio Blaster, Watcher, Seeker). Its trial figures below are preliminary
54-specialist harness evidence, not complete production ecology evidence. The
final harness loads those actual modules, checking all66 templates and the same
exposure floors. No copied selection stubs stand in for their eligibility.

Theme tuning changed from reciprocal count weights to uniform selection among
eligible least-used themes after the first measured campaign failure. The final
scoring also ignores ordinary Shambler/Runner/Soldier escorts when applying
specialist novelty penalties; otherwise ubiquitous companions mask family and
enemy novelty. Common fallback squads still score all their identities.
