# Looking at another player

Aim at a living, visible player to see two lines below the crosshair:

```text
Username as Character Name
current/max HP
```

The username is identity-blue, `as` is neutral and the character name is
character-red, matching LoD's shared information colors. A human-controlled
Soldier appears as `Username as Soldier`, not their inactive Hero's name.

The health fraction remains resource-green. Only `HP` changes: green at full
health or above; yellow below full but above half; orange above one-quarter
through half; red at one-quarter or less. Exact values remain visible, so color
is supplementary. An unavailable maximum is shown as `?` with a neutral `HP`.

Long names shorten with an ellipsis instead of creating a third line. The
readout hides for menus, dead or hidden targets and active Veil concealment;
stock GMod nickname/health-percent information does not return underneath it.
