# GPS navigation praise — eight-role opinion mixer

Author request: GPS corrects wrong turns almost immediately and supplies frequent,
comically excessive encouragement when the Hero makes progress toward the current
objective. These are the established fictional editorial personas, not real-world
contributors. This document supplies authored lines; it does not set cadence or
claim runtime acceptance.

## Compact room pass

- Roxy Bellows, club comic: praise the mundane physical act until ankles seem
  worthy of ovation. Contributions: 8 and 10.
- Perry Quibble, social friction: make the approval embarrassingly public and
  familial, without making the player owe the GPS anything. Contributions: 2 and 14.
- Mira Aside, outsider satire: let a navigation instrument confess that its
  confidence is partly emotional. Contributions: 3 and 9.
- Kit Glitch, meta satire: the disembodied system yearns for a heart and hands;
  keep that yearning brief and affectionate. Contributions: 6 and 11.
- Bea Bunch, ensemble continuity: warmth belongs to the player's present progress,
  without borrowing the Hermit's identity or presuming a rescue. Contributions: 1
  and 12.
- Ada Branch, clue legibility: plainly name correct direction and objective
  progress; comedy must not obscure the navigational verdict. Contributions: 4
  and 7.
- Quinn Keyes, assembly: alternate plain approval, physical comedy and excessive
  attachment; add 5, 13, 15 and 16 to complete the sixteen-line bank.
- Ada's truthful-clue veto: these lines belong only to verified progress toward
  the current objective. Remove any promise of safety, rewards, imminent arrival,
  future objectives, loot or enemy information. Do not imply that maintaining the
  present heading will remain correct after the next turn. Changed “every step”
  to “your latest step” so a correct move does not certify previous wrong turns.
- Ned Still, final restraint: one short beat per line; no lore exposition,
  punchline chains or sarcasm aimed at the Hero. The joke is the GPS's inexhaustible
  pride.
