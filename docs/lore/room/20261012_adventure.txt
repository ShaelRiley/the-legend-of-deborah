Proposed Hermit beat script (one paragraph per staging Dungeon Level)
1. 'Welcome to the refuge. The women of the Debbie Fund used to keep this place supplied; Gordon has imprisoned them. Nessa is first. Move with WASD, aim with the mouse, attack with left mouse and reload with R; right mouse casts your selected Magic. E uses what you face. P chooses your class and first feat; O opens Equipment, I the Spellbook, Q or M the Magic-powered map. Take your pedestal weapon before the blue portal. First deployment starts thirty minutes, before any time allowance. Follow Red, Blue, Yellow, then Black; Neil carries Black. Defeat Gordon, collect the Jail Key, open the chamber and reach Nessa. One rescue starts the next.'
2. 'Nessa is home; Bessa is next. Neither owes you a performance of gratitude. Before Gordon seized the network, their ammunition went where it was needed. There are worse names for that than a fund.'
3. 'Tessa kept the emergency stores. She called preparation optimism with pockets. Gordon calls it contraband whenever the wrong people can defend themselves.'
4. 'Odessa demanded that everyone learn which end of a weapon offers the argument. The Warden prefers a helpless captive. It photographs better.'
5. 'Nell knows the difference between an order and an excuse. Neil carries the Black Keycard; he is not Nell. Gordon has found employment for every confusion he can manufacture.'
6. 'Belle treats wounds without asking who deserved them. That offended Gordon: he considers suffering an excellent admissions test.'
7. 'Estelle removes the labyrinth's ailments. Gordon wishes every injury to become a permanent character trait. She is professionally opposed.'
8. 'Mirabelle taught us that Magic is a resource, not a moral rank. Even your map spends it. Knowledge is useful; keeping it open forever is expensive.'
9. 'May supplied the working hands. Look around this refuge: the rescued do not simply disappear when their chapter is finished. They are rebuilding the place while you advance.'
10. 'Faye kept the circle together. A ring is a fine symbol, but she prefers something that actually helps. Gordon loves symbols; they cost less than helping.'
11. 'Kay prepared routes out. The containers rearrange, but a prison remains a prison however impressive its architecture. Do not mistake another corridor for another destiny.'
12. 'Desiree recorded what each person could do. Not what they were worth. The Director's paperwork is less careful about that distinction.'
13. 'Jean wanted everyone clothed against the consequences. The stock survived the seizure. So did this refuge. Someone clearly wanted rescuers to keep arriving.'
14. 'Colleen hated uniforms that make individuals vanish. The guards have their parts; you need not accept yours. A hero is allowed to want something besides applause.'
15. 'Maureen kept the shields ready. I called her cautious. She asked why my plans always required somebody else's bruises. An unfairly memorable question.'
16. 'Josephine saved the emergencies for the medicine, rather than the medicine for emergencies worth watching. I may have encouraged the opposite. A little tension sharpens a story.'
17. 'Dora says healing is not payment for being interesting. I used to disagree. Do not let the labyrinth convince you that pain is the only proof you have done something.'
18. 'Cora kept the rescue fund's accounts. The women meant it to support people, not to purchase their obedience. The Warden stole the resources. Someone else stole the narrative.'
19. 'Eleonora believes another chance is a responsibility, not a prize. The labyrinth can make a hostile skeleton from a fallen body; that copy is not the friend who returns to you. I have been rather fond of copies. Easier to direct.'
20. 'Deborah founded a refuge, not a casting office. She wants people free to leave, to stay, to choose. I thought a legend required someone arranging the danger. Reach her after Gordon falls. You may find the arrangement has one last objection.'

Deborah, post20
'Hector used our refuge as his stage. He wore the Hermit's face and fed you enough truth to keep you moving. Gordon's letter is quite candid: now he steals cash for the love of the game. Those bags are stolen resources; recovering them keeps taking something out of his hands. Nobody else is waiting to be rescued in a bag. Stay if you want to challenge him; the rescue is won. Our support does not require another performance. The pedestal keeps its usual gifts, and Abundance remains one token each day under the existing claim rules.'

Vetoes
Do not claim all services are unlimited/unconditional in frequency, all characters return forever, all skeletons are dead teammates, wallet money is necessarily identical to the cash-case tally, or defeat of Hector permanently removes Gordon. No forced conversational prerequisites, money-for-dialogue, dialogue timer extension, new named Cash, or story-driven conversion of aid into wages. Any rescue-network fiction belongs in GDD before code. Current services have distinct visit/campaign/eligibility limits; retain those while expressing unconditional dignity rather than unlimited grants. Story should never imply safe menus during active deployment.
