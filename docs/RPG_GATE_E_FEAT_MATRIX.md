This matrix is the Gate E completeness ledger. The live GDD remains authoritative. **73 ordinary feats:** 6 implemented across batches 1–2, 17 represented as catalog/ownership only, and 50 not yet catalogued at this commit. The six permanent neutral fallback cards and nine Level-20 class capstones are separate catalogs and are not included in the 73.

Direct Feat→Perk prerequisites do not exist in the authored catalog. Perks will compose with Feats only through the shared semantic authority named in the final column (damage, movement, reload, Magic, Morale, spotting, navigation, and so forth). At this checkpoint, identity perks remain ownership/display data except for permanent identity ability deltas; therefore every non-ability Perk interaction remains pending for the Perk Gate rather than being silently credited as implemented here.

## Batch rule

A status may advance to **Implemented + validator** only when the effect reaches its canonical gameplay seam, rank/prerequisite behavior is enforced, the Character Sheet reflects runtime truth, and a finite developer validator covers the bridge. Catalog presence or feat ownership alone is not implementation.
