[h1]THE LEGEND OF DEBORAH[/h1]
[b]Public Alpha - a procedural cooperative action-RPG labyrinth for Garry's Mod[/b]

Deborah is trapped somewhere inside an impossible freight-container labyrinth built over gm_flatgrass. Enter as a Hero, descend through a newly generated maze, fight through escalating threats, find the keys, survive the Wardens, rescue Deborah - and then do it again as the labyrinth rebuilds itself.

Every dungeon is procedurally generated. Routes, floors, gates, encounters, loot, vertical transitions, enemy composition, and rewards change from run to run while the server validates that progression remains solvable.

[b]The current build is vastly larger than the original Workshop alpha.[/b]

[h2]PROCEDURAL WEAPON APPEARANCE[/h2]
Generated weapon properties now alter their appearance through partial finishes, procedural surface treatments, elemental coloration, restrained glow/aura effects, and muzzle presentation while preserving the native Source weapon silhouette.

[h2]EXPANDED ENEMY ROSTER[/h2]
The labyrinth now contains Shamblers, Runners, Soldiers, Deadcrabs, Bio Blasters, Climbers, Nodules, Flamers, Big Crabs, Sentries, Razors, Arc Casters, Lurkers, Beam Sweepers, and other specialized threats.

Enemies use the procedural maze graph for high-level navigation through the dynamically generated container complex.

[h2]NEIL, THE BRUTE, AND THE BLACK KEYCARD[/h2]
Late-dungeon progression includes a multi-floor hunt for Neil and his Brute protector. The Brute escorts Neil, reacts to threats, pursues surviving Heroes, and commits to a telegraphed charge with wall-impact counterplay. The hunt advances the Black Keycard sequence and deeper Core progression.

[h2]GORDON THE WARDEN[/h2]
The final Warden is a multi-phase boss encounter in a dedicated multi-level arena. Gordon uses a stock citizen presentation with a procedural pig mask - no external model dependency - and brings pursuit, ranged pressure, phase escalation, reinforcements, protected co-op respawns, and Jail Key progression.

[h2]30-MINUTE DUNGEON COLLAPSE[/h2]
Once the Heroes deploy, the active dungeon has 30 minutes remaining. Rescue Deborah before TIME OVER and the labyrinth's cinematic destruction sequence.

A successful rescue resets the next dungeon clock to 30:00. It stays paused during intermission, generation, and staging, then starts when the next Hero deploys.
